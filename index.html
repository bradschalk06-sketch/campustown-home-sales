05 · Methodology

How the numbers are calculated

Every figure on this site is computed in your browser from data/site_sales.csv, which contains all … rows of the original dataset (data/college_town_home_sales.csv) with only the columns the site needs. No numbers are typed in by hand. Medians use the middle value (or the average of the two middle values when the count is even).

Price rule

Sales below $10,000 or above $5,000,000 are treated as implausible and left out of every statistic and chart: … sales under $10,000 and … over $5,000,000. That leaves an analysis set of … sales. The explorer can still show the excluded rows.

Price per square foot

Sale price divided by finished living area, only where living area is recorded and greater than zero. … rows have no living area and … report 0, so price per square foot uses … sales.

Days on market

Contract date minus list date, the way listing services count it, not the closing date. A missing date is never counted as zero: the figure uses only the … sales in the analysis set that have both dates (…). Homes sold without a listing, often new construction, are not represented, so coverage is shown beside each figure.

Identifiers and duplicates

listing_id is the unique key (… unique ids for … rows). Nothing is deduplicated by street address: separate condo units in one building share an address and are distinct sales. ZIP codes are read as text.

Property type

The source's home_type field labels nearly every row single family, including condo units, so it is not used anywhere on this site.

Campus areas

Each sale belongs to the nearest of 11 campus landmarks, by its own coordinates. miles_to_campus is straight-line distance. Champaign, Urbana and Savoy make up the University of Illinois area.
