05 · Methodology

About the data and methods

The figures in this report are drawn from the College-town Home Sales dataset, version 1.0, which records … completed home sales near 11 university campuses between … and …. All statistics are calculated directly from these records. Medians are reported rather than averages because home prices and selling times are skewed by a small number of very high values; where a group contains an even number of sales, the median is the average of the two middle values.

Price range

The analysis is limited to sales between $10,000 and $5,000,000. This range is an analytical choice that keeps extreme recorded prices, which run from $0 to more than $80 million, from distorting market comparisons; it does not imply that the excluded transactions are invalid. The range excludes … sales below $10,000 and … above $5,000,000, leaving … sales in the analysis. Excluded sales remain available in the Property Explorer.

Price per square foot

Sale price divided by finished living area, calculated only where living area is reported and greater than zero. … sales have no reported living area and … report zero, so price per square foot is based on … sales.

Days on market

Measured from the listing date to the contract date, consistent with industry practice, rather than to the closing date. Sales missing either date are excluded rather than counted as zero, leaving … sales (…). Homes sold without a public listing, often new construction, are therefore not represented, and coverage is reported alongside each figure.

Identifiers and duplicates

Each sale carries a unique listing identifier (… identifiers for … sales). Sales are not combined by street address: separate condominium units in the same building can share an address and are counted as distinct sales.

Property type

The source's property-type field classifies nearly every sale as single-family, including condominium units, so it is not used in any comparison.

Campus areas

Each sale is assigned to the nearest of 11 campus landmarks based on the property's coordinates, and distance to campus is measured in a straight line. Champaign, Urbana and Savoy make up the University of Illinois area.

Data source and citation

College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.

Values are as reported by the collection source and have not been independently verified against county records. Detailed methodology notes, including independent checks of key figures, accompany the published data.
